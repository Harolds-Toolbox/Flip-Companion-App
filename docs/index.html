Install in four steps

  1. 01 · DownloadTap the green download button on your Android phone.
  2. 02 · InstallOpen the downloaded APK. If Android asks, allow your browser or Files app to install from this source, then confirm Install.
  3. 03 · Allow accessOpen PABI Flip Companion, choose a calling app, and tap Enable permissions. Turn on notification access, then tap Check permissions. Instagram, Snapchat, and Discord also need Accessibility access for Answer on open.
  4. 04 · Choose behaviourTurn on Answer on open or Hang up on close as you prefer. Discord has one pair of controls for both voice and video calls.

Notification access blocked?

On some Android phones, notification access is a restricted setting. To enable access, open Settings → Apps → PABI Flip Companion → ⋮ → Allow restricted settings. Then return to notification access.

Compatibility and privacy

Built for supported calling apps on a Galaxy Z Flip with a hinge-angle sensor. Tested on a Samsung zFlip 8. WhatsApp Business and other flip models have not been verified.

Android grants notification listeners broad access. This app filters for supported call notifications, stores no raw notification text, and has no Internet permission. Discord may require a passcode before it can join a call.
